TAG • Trigger Antidote Gambit

Privacy policy

Offline version with bots · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Italiano · English
The offline game stores your profile and preferences on your device. It does not send this information to the developer and does not include ads, in-app purchases or online accounts.

1. Responsible developer and contact

Giuseppe Papa is responsible for TAG • Trigger Antidote Gambit, Android package com.propinosoftware.tag.

For privacy questions, requests about your data or customer support, contact propinosoftware@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the game

The app stores the following information locally to remember your choices:

  • your nickname, selected avatar and initial setup completion;
  • language, audio volume and mute preferences;
  • whether you completed or skipped the tutorial.

Matches run locally against bots. Your nickname is displayed within the game on your device. Quick messages are not sent to other real users.

In versions displaying a support ID in settings, this identifier is generated and stored locally. You can copy it to the clipboard by pressing its button. This action does not automatically send it to the developer, and the ID is not an online account.

3. Collection, sharing and third-party services

The offline version does not transmit your profile, preferences or match results to developer servers or third-party services. It does not sell user data.

Authentication, Firebase, advertising, in-app purchasing, player behaviour analytics and remote game diagnostics services are not active. The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Installation and updates through Google Play, as well as any device backup features, are system or platform services governed by their own settings and privacy policies.

4. Retention, security and deletion

Game information remains in the app’s local storage to remember your preferences. You can change your nickname, avatar, language and audio through the settings available in the game.

You can delete local information through Android settings for the app by clearing its data or storage. This resets the profile, preferences and tutorial status. Any system backups are managed separately through device and account settings.

Local storage protection also depends on operating system and device security. The developer does not retain a remote copy of your profile and cannot recover it from a server.

5. Email requests

If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide, such as your nickname, support ID, issue description or attachments. We use this information to handle your request and reply, not for advertising.

Messages are managed through Gmail, provided by Google, and retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and any applicable obligations. For processing by the email provider, see Google’s privacy policy.

You may use the same contact address to request information or exercise applicable rights, including access, correction and deletion of information received through support. Where applicable, you may contact the relevant data protection authority.

6. Visiting this page

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub privacy statement.

This page’s code includes no analytics tools, trackers, data collection forms or developer-set cookies. Visiting the website is separate from playing the game offline.

7. Game audience

TAG is designed for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older. The offline version applies the local practices described in this policy to all players and does not require sending a date of birth or account information.

8. Policy updates

This policy describes the offline game. If we introduce online features, accounts, ads, in-app purchases or new data processing practices, we will update this policy and Google Play declarations before distributing those features. Where required, we will provide specific information and consent mechanisms.

The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
