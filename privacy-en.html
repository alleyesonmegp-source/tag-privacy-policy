TAG offers local bot matches, cloud accounts and online multiplayer. Online features send necessary information to TAG and Google/Firebase services. Technical update checks may contact Firebase even before you link an account.

1. Responsible developer and contact

Giuseppe Papa is responsible for TAG • Trigger Antidote Gambit, Android package com.propinosoftware.tag. For customer support, privacy and data requests: propinosoftware@gmail.com.

2. Local data, accounts and sessions

The device stores your nickname, selected preset avatar, language, audio, tutorial and initial setup state. Bot matches run locally.

Cloud saving associates your Firebase identifier with a profile: support ID, nickname, avatar, dates and revision. It can use a Firebase guest identity or Google. An unlinked cloud guest may not be recoverable after reinstallation or loss of local data.

Google and Firebase handle sign-in and identity details, which can include an identifier, email, name and picture. The game does not receive your Google password. Random installation and session identifiers, distinct from an IMEI or hardware serial, manage the session held by a device.

3. Online matches and visibility

The server receives actions, position in the virtual arena, card choices, round state, outcomes and quick-message identifiers. Game coordinates are not geographical location. Messages are preset phrases: there is no free-text or voice chat.

Other participants see your chosen nickname, avatar and information provided by the rules. Do not put personal information in a nickname. Your Google name and picture are separate from your game nickname and avatar; private choices are intended only for the recipients defined by the game.

5. Retention, security and transfers

Your cloud profile remains until deletion through the account flow or verified support request. Session data is updated or removed during use, session release or account deletion, without a guaranteed fixed automatic expiry. Signing out, uninstalling or clearing Android data does not automatically delete your cloud profile. Local preferences and Android backups are managed separately.

Online room state is held in working memory and removed during room cleanup or closure; abuse controls maintain temporary counters. This does not guarantee that every provider retains no technical logs.

Firestore uses daily backups retained for 7 days and PITR versions for up to 7 days. Existing copies or versions may retain information until their normal expiry after active-service deletion. Provider technical data follows their terms; we do not promise instant deletion from every system.

Cloud requests use encrypted HTTPS/WSS and access controls. They require no contacts, GPS, camera or microphone access. Google may process information outside the European Economic Area; Authentication operates in the United States. Applicable conditions and safeguards are in the Firebase processing terms. A European server region does not confine every service to Europe.

6. Deletion and rights

In the app: Settings → Account → DELETE ACCOUNT, confirm and complete any reauthentication with the same Google account. The cloud profile and session are removed and deletion of the Firebase identity is requested. These operations are separate: an error can leave a part to complete through support. Your Google account is not deleted.

You can also start a request without reinstalling from the dedicated page below. Clear local data through Android settings. Contact us for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability or withdrawal of consent. We verify ownership using only necessary information. You can contact the competent data-protection authority.
