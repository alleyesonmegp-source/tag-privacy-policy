Terms of Use · Privacy notice · Account deletion
You can request deletion of your TAG account and associated data without reinstalling the app. Your Google account is not deleted.

1. Request deletion by email

Email propinosoftware@gmail.com with the subject TAG account deletion request, stating that you want the account and associated cloud profile deleted. This is also the customer-support address.

Where possible, use the linked Google email and include a nickname or support ID you already know. If you no longer have access, or used a cloud guest, describe the issue: we will explain the minimum information needed to find the account and verify ownership. Reinstallation is not needed to start. The link opens your email program and does not send automatically. We do not request passwords, tokens, access codes or payment details.

2. Or use the app

Open the profile to delete, then Settings → Account → DELETE ACCOUNT. Confirm and complete any reauthentication using the same Google account. If an error appears, contact support to complete the request.

3. Deleted data and retained copies

The request concerns the TAG cloud profile (nickname, avatar, identifiers and technical profile details), its session and Firebase identity. Profile/session and authentication operations are separate; a partial failure may need support. Your Google account is not deleted.

Active-service deletion does not immediately erase existing copies: daily Firestore backups and PITR versions can retain them until expiry within the configured 7-day window. Provider technical data and request communications follow the policy’s retention information. To clear local preferences too, clear Android app data; that action alone does not delete your cloud profile.
